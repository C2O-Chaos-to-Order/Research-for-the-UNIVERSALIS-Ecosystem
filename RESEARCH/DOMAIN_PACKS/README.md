## Status discipline

- Mathematical combination counts are exact; element names and semantics are proposed ontology content.
- Tool and vendor claims are source assertions until reproduced.
- Openly documented is not synonymous with open source.
- Publicly available does not mean safe to run or authorized to target.
- A signature, ledger, consensus, or score proves only the property defined by its protocol and validation method.
- No package implies product implementation, acceptance, certification, or release authorization.

These domain packs are research inputs. Promotion to canonical ontology content or product implementation requires independent review, source/version/license qualification, traceability, and explicit approval.
