**Research status:** Active research workspace; working materials are preliminary.  
**Document date:** 2026-11-02

## Purpose

The Golden Compass research programme examines how people, communities, institutions, and societies make consequential decisions; how historical circumstances shape those decisions; how benefits and burdens are distributed; and how harmful outcomes can be prevented, corrected, and remedied.

The programme studies humanity as a whole, not only rulers or people who held formal authority. It includes political and institutional leaders, reformers, resistance movements, scientists, philosophers, educators, inventors, humanitarian workers, Indigenous nations, communities, witnesses, survivors, and people whose lives were affected by decisions made by others.

**Research informs design. Evidence supports claims. Separate authorization governs implementation and release.**

## Foundational philosophy: Shared Agency

The project owner supplied the following originating statement on 2026-11-02:

> “We're all in this together, or we aren't truly in it at all.”

This statement is recorded as the project's founding Shared Agency philosophy. Its working interpretation is that knowledge, opportunity, useful capabilities, and meaningful participation should be broadly accessible; decisions should account for affected people; and collective progress must preserve individual dignity, autonomy, and the right to disagree.

Shared agency is not forced consensus, uniformity, collective ownership of individuals, or unrestricted distribution of authority. Empowerment is not domination. Knowledge of power does not confer authority to exercise it over others.

See [PRINCIPLES/SHARED-AGENCY.md](PRINCIPLES/SHARED-AGENCY.md).

## Research tracks

The programme connects the following tracks:

1. **Historical leadership and governance** — presidents, monarchs, elected officials, judges, military commanders, and institutional leaders.
2. **Civilization, society, and collective agency** — communities, cultures, social movements, Indigenous nations, and collective governance.
3. **Knowledge, discovery, and innovation** — scientists, philosophers, physicians, educators, inventors, artists, and researchers.
4. **Human rights, reform, and resistance** — abolitionists, civil-rights advocates, dissidents, whistleblowers, labor organizers, and reform movements.
5. **Human relationships and cooperation** — trust, influence, dependency, loyalty, disagreement, conflict, and reconciliation.
6. **Historical suffering, service, and recovery** — affected populations, survivors, humanitarian action, and community rebuilding.
7. **Decision determinants and global context** — the conditions, information, incentives, relationships, constraints, and worldwide events surrounding decisions.
8. **Preventive governance and intelligence applications** — translating supported historical lessons into bounded, reviewable, testable requirements.

These tracks overlap. No individual or community is reduced to a single category.

## Research method

All case studies use the common methods in [METHODS.md](METHODS.md), including:

- Global Historical Context Analysis (GC-R02-GHC).
- Decision Determinants Analysis (GC-R02-DDA).
- Affected-party and agency mapping.
- Contemporaneous-knowledge reconstruction.
- Alternative and consequence analysis.
- Evidence provenance, uncertainty, disagreement, and correction records.
- Explicit separation of research, simulation, review, verification, implementation, and authorization.

## Status and governance boundaries

- This repository is the public UNIVERSALIS research workspace.
- Research artifacts do not establish implementation, deployment, legal authority, or permission to act.
- No claim is treated as verified merely because it appears in a working paper.
- No simulation credit is awarded for a description, hypothetical scenario, or tabletop discussion.
- The programme does not rank political figures or societies. It evaluates specific decisions, processes, evidence, consequences, and contested interpretations.
- The same evidentiary and ethical standards apply to every individual, party, institution, culture, and system, including UNIVERSALIS and its creators.
- Private C2O material, personal information, credentials, and unapproved internal material do not belong in this public repository.

## Change control

Foundational statements, proposed principles, research findings, and implementation requirements must retain distinct statuses. Amendments must be traceable and must not silently convert a proposal into an adopted constitutional rule.

**Guiding distinction:** Understanding humanity does not confer authority over humanity.
