## assumptions

X is the real plane and Y the real line, all quantities dimensionless, d_Y the absolute difference, and phi the arithmetic mean, matching the tablet's own worked example in section 5. Delta is taken as F_Y(phi(x)) minus phi(F_X(x)) in absolute value. All arithmetic is exact rationals, so no floating-point tolerance is involved. The declared domain is treated as a closed box in both coordinates.

## acceptance_or_falsification_criteria

Acceptance: error is zero at every step and never exceeds the bound. Falsification: any nonzero error. A pass establishes only that the harness runs, since the bound is trivially satisfied.

## actual_conditions

Deterministic exact-rational computation. No network, no random sampling, no external service. Cases run in separate subprocesses against a hash-locked model file.

## outcome_summary

Error was zero at all six sampled steps and the bound was satisfied throughout. The result is trivial by construction and carries no evidential weight about the bound's usefulness.
