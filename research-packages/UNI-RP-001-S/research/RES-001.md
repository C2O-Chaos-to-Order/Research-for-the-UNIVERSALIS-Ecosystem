## objective_question

If the comparison error starts at zero and the family never moves, does the error stay at zero so the additive envelope holds in a trivial way?

## method_summary

Iterate the still family from (2, 2) with z = 2 for five steps and compare the error to n * Delta with Delta = 0.

## acceptance_or_falsification_criteria

Acceptance: error is zero at every sampled step and never exceeds the bound. Falsification: any nonzero error.

## outcome_summary

Error was zero at all six sampled steps and the bound held throughout. The result is trivial by construction and is only a harness check.
