## objective_question

Is the additive envelope merely valid, or is it attained by a family with constant drive, so that a smaller coefficient would fail?

## method_summary

Iterate the steady-drive family with Delta = 1/5 for five steps and compare the error to n * Delta at each step.

## acceptance_or_falsification_criteria

Acceptance: the error equals the bound at every step. Falsification: any step where the error is strictly below the bound.

## outcome_summary

The error equalled the bound at every step: 0, 1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5, 1. The additive form is sharp for this family.

## uncertainty_and_limitations

Sharpness is shown for a constant drive only. A state-dependent drive is not tested.

## open_obligations

Test sharpness for a drive that varies with the state.
